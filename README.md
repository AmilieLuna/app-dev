# My Favorite Series and Movies
I really enjoy **thrillers** and *dark films about humans* that explore psychology, survival, and moral choices. Two of my all-time favorites are **Dexter** and the **Saw** movie franchise.

---

## Favorite Series: Dexter
**Dexter** follows Dexter Morgan, a forensic blood spatter analyst who leads a secret double life. 
### Why I Like Dexter
* Deep psychological storytelling and suspense
* Intense cat-and-mouse chases in every season
* Complex human characters and moral dilemmas
> "Tonight's the night."

---

## Favorite Movies: Saw Franchise
The **Saw** movies focus on survival tests created by Jigsaw to see how far people will go to appreciate their lives.
### Top 3 Reasons I Enjoy Saw
1. Clever plot twists that connect across the movies
2. Intense suspense and problem-solving under pressure
3. Dark exploration of human nature and choices
